# Signing Up

To sign up, a customer needs to be at least 18 years old, live in one of the four Tretwerk cities, and have a bank account in the European Union for SEPA direct debit. Credit cards are not accepted.

During sign-up, customers verify their identity with a video call or with the online ID check in the app. The ID check usually takes less than ten minutes. A refundable deposit of 100 euros is charged with the first payment and paid back within 14 days after the bike is returned in good condition.

Students get 10 percent off the Urban and Fold plans with a valid student ID. The discount does not apply to the Cargo plan.
