# Cancelling a Subscription

Customers can cancel at any time in the app or by email, with a notice period of 14 days. The subscription ends 14 days after the cancellation request, not at the end of the month. The last monthly payment is calculated per day.

After cancelling, the customer books a pickup appointment. The bike, the charger, both keys and any rented accessories must be returned at the pickup. A missing key costs 30 euros, a missing charger 75 euros.

Customers on an annual plan can cancel too. They get back the payment for the full months that remain, minus the two free months they received.
