# Privacy and GPS

Every Tretwerk bike has a GPS tracker built into the frame. The tracker is only used to find a bike after a reported theft or when a payment is overdue and the motor is locked. Tretwerk does not use the location data for marketing and does not track rides.

Location data is deleted after 30 days. Customers can request a copy of the data stored about them by writing to privacy@tretwerk.example; Tretwerk replies within one month.
