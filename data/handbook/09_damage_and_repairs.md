# Damage and Repairs

All repairs caused by normal wear are free: brake pads, tires, chains, lights and the battery. Customers report a problem in the app, and a technician repairs the bike at the customer's door within three working days.

Damage caused by an accident, misuse or vandalism is charged to the customer. Common examples:

| Damage | Charge |
|---|---|
| Broken display | 120 euros |
| Bent front wheel | 95 euros |
| Damaged rear rack | 60 euros |
| Scratched or dented frame | no charge |

If the bike cannot be repaired within three working days, the customer gets a replacement bike for free. The charge for a single accident is capped at 300 euros per bike.
