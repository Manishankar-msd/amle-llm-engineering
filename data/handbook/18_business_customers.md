# Business Customers

Companies can rent bikes for their employees with a Tretwerk Business plan. The minimum is five bikes, and the contract runs for at least twelve months. Business customers get one invoice per month for all bikes and a dedicated account manager.

Prices for business plans are agreed individually. Support agents do not quote business prices; they forward the request to the business team at business@tretwerk.example.

Employees of business customers report repairs and thefts like private customers, through the app.
