# Subscription Plans

Tretwerk offers three plans. Prices are per month and include VAT.

| Plan | Bike | Price per month |
|---|---|---|
| Urban | Tretwerk Urban U2 | 49 euros |
| Fold | Tretwerk Fold F3 | 59 euros |
| Cargo | Tretwerk Cargo C1 | 89 euros |

Every plan has a minimum term of one month. Customers who pay for twelve months in advance get two months free, so an annual Urban plan costs 490 euros.

Customers can switch to another plan once per calendar month. The new price applies from the day the new bike is delivered. Switching is free, but the old bike has to be returned at the same appointment.
