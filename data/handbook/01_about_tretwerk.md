# About Tretwerk

Tretwerk rents e-bikes by monthly subscription in four German cities: Berlin, Hamburg, Munich and Cologne. The subscription includes the bike, delivery, all maintenance and repairs from normal wear, and customer support. Customers never own the bike; they return it when the subscription ends.

Tretwerk was founded in Berlin in 2019 and has about 21,000 active subscriptions. The support team answers customers by email, chat and phone. This handbook is the reference for everything support agents tell customers. If a question is not covered here, agents ask the team lead instead of guessing.
