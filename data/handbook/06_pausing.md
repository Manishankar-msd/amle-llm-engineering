# Pausing a Subscription

Instead of cancelling, customers can pause their subscription for up to two months per calendar year, for example during winter or a long trip. A pause must last at least two weeks.

During a pause, Tretwerk picks up the bike and stores it. The customer pays a reduced fee of 9 euros per month for storage. The pause can be requested in the app at least five working days before it should start.

When the pause ends, the customer gets the same bike back, freshly serviced. Pausing does not count as cancelling, so the deposit stays with Tretwerk.
