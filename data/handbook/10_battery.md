# Battery and Charging

A full battery gives a range of about 70 kilometers on the Urban U2 and the Fold F3, and about 50 kilometers on the Cargo C1 with a full load. Range drops by up to 30 percent below 5 degrees Celsius.

Charging from empty takes about four hours with the Tretwerk charger. Customers should only use the charger that came with the bike. The battery can be charged on the bike or removed with the frame key.

In winter, the battery should be stored and charged indoors. A battery that is older than three years or that loses its charge quickly is replaced for free.
