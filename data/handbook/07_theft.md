# Theft

If a bike is stolen, the customer must report the theft to the police within 48 hours and upload the police report in the app. Without a police report, Tretwerk charges the full replacement value of the bike: 1,800 euros for the Urban U2, 2,100 euros for the Fold F3 and 3,900 euros for the Cargo C1.

With a police report, the customer pays a deductible of 450 euros. Customers who booked theft protection pay a deductible of 90 euros instead.

The bike must have been locked with the frame lock and attached to a fixed object. If the keys were stolen together with the bike, theft protection does not apply. A replacement bike is delivered within five working days.
