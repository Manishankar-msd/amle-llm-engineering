# Bike Models

**Tretwerk Urban U2.** A city bike with a 250-watt rear hub motor, seven gears and a weight of 23 kilograms. It suits riders from 160 to 195 centimeters tall.

**Tretwerk Fold F3.** A folding bike with 20-inch wheels and a weight of 19 kilograms. Folded, it fits into the trunk of a small car and can be taken on trains for free in most German cities.

**Tretwerk Cargo C1.** A cargo bike with a front box for up to 80 kilograms, for example two children or shopping. The box has seat belts for two children and a rain cover. The Cargo C1 weighs 45 kilograms and needs a parking space on the ground floor or in a courtyard.

All models have a maximum assisted speed of 25 kilometers per hour, as required by German law.
