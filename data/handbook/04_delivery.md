# Delivery and First Ride

Tretwerk delivers the bike to the customer's door within five working days after sign-up. Customers choose a two-hour delivery window in the app. Delivery is free.

At delivery, the technician adjusts the saddle and handlebar height, explains the display and the charger, and hands over two keys for the frame lock. The customer signs the handover protocol in the app, which records the frame number and any existing scratches.

If the customer is not at home during the delivery window, the technician waits ten minutes. A second delivery attempt costs 25 euros.
