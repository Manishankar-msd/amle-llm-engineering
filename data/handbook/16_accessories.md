# Accessories

Customers can rent accessories in the app. They are delivered with the bike or at the next service appointment.

| Accessory | Price per month |
|---|---|
| Child seat (rear, up to 22 kg) | 6 euros |
| Second frame lock | 3 euros |
| Phone holder | 2 euros |
| Cargo box rain cover (Cargo C1 only) | included |

Helmets are not rented for hygiene reasons, but customers get 20 percent off helmets in the partner shop. Rented accessories are returned together with the bike when the subscription ends.
