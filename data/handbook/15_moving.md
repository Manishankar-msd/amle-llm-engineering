# Moving to Another City

Customers who move between two Tretwerk cities keep their subscription. They report the move in the app at least ten working days in advance. Tretwerk picks up the bike at the old address and delivers a bike of the same model at the new address. The move is free once per year; a second move in the same year costs 49 euros.

If a customer moves to a city without Tretwerk, the subscription has to be cancelled with the usual notice period of 14 days. Tretwerk does not ship bikes to other cities or countries.
