# Payments and Invoices

The monthly price is collected by SEPA direct debit on the first working day of each month. Invoices are available in the app as PDF files.

If a payment fails, Tretwerk tries again after five days. A failed payment costs a fee of 5 euros. After two failed payments in a row, the bike's motor is locked remotely until the outstanding amount is paid. Customers can pay outstanding amounts by bank transfer.

Customers can change their bank account in the app at any time. The change applies to the next payment.
