# Theft Protection

Theft protection is an add-on that costs 8 euros per month. It can be booked at sign-up or later in the app and starts on the first day of the next month.

With theft protection, the deductible after a theft drops from 450 euros to 90 euros. It also covers the theft of rented accessories such as the child seat and the cargo box.

Theft protection does not cover damage from accidents or vandalism; see the page on damage and repairs. It can be cancelled at the end of any month.
