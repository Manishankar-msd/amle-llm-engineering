# Service Appointments

Every bike gets a free inspection every six months. Tretwerk sends a reminder in the app two weeks before the inspection is due, and the customer chooses a two-hour slot. The technician comes to the customer's address, and the inspection takes about 45 minutes.

Customers can move an appointment for free up to 24 hours before it starts. A missed appointment without notice costs 25 euros, the same as a second delivery attempt.

Repairs and inspections are carried out by Tretwerk technicians only. Customers must not take the bike to another bike shop; the cost of outside repairs is not refunded.
