# Customer Support

Customers reach Tretwerk support in the app chat, by email at help@tretwerk.example, or by phone. Chat and phone are open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 to 20:00. Emails are answered within one working day.

For accidents with injuries, customers call the emergency number 112 first. A breakdown on the road is not an emergency: the customer locks the bike, reports the problem in the app, and a technician collects or repairs the bike within three working days.

Agents can grant a goodwill credit of up to 20 euros without asking the team lead, for example after a late delivery.
